## What is not ours to license

Some files were made by others and keep their own terms. The attribution ledger, `assets/LEDGER.yaml`,
is the authority for every file under `assets/`: it names each file's origin, author, source and
licence. In short:

- **Battle-effect pictures** (`assets/fx/`): Unity Labs' free VFX flipbooks, CC0 1.0.
- **Sound effects** (`assets/sounds/`): recordings from Freesound, each CC0 1.0, each credited in the ledger.
- **Fonts** shipped with the client: each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, as its ledger entry says.

Where a ledger entry says a file's licence is "unstated", the terms of the service it was made with
have not been recorded yet; the notice above states the owner's intent for those files and does not
override the terms of that service.

## What is not in this licence at all

or table. The game is an original design.
